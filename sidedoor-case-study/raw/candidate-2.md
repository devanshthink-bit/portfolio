1.Do you have any experience as a job seeker or to put it simply, have you ever asked somebody to refer you in some company? Yes, I have.

2.How do you currently get job referrals? So basically, if there's a friend of mine who's working in some XYZ company, I directly ask them, or cold messaging on LinkedIn, or via Fishbowl sometimes, that works.

3.What is Fishbowl. Can you elaborate? So basically, Fishbowl is similar to Glassdoor and AmbitionBox. You can check people, they're working on companies. For example, if you're working in some XYZ company and I am working at ABC, so I can see that you're working in that company and I can message you to get some information about that company or to get a referral. It do not share any personal details but yeah, we can communicate on it via messaging.

4. Is it meant for referrals only? what is its basic purpose? No, so basically on Glassdoor also we have many groups or communities in which you can ask for referral, you can give referral, you can join communities which on Glassdoor, and similarly you have on Fishbowl. So basically it's an alternative app from Glassdoor and AmbitionBox. You can see what is the market, how is the market, how is the environment in some particular company, stuff like that.

5. So basically it's a community platform where people can interact and you can ask for any kind of help, be it referral or something else? Yes, where you can get in touch with people. But as per my experience, it's not very useful or very efficient because you do not get anybody's personal information, which is great, but then if you connect to somebody, not everybody is using Fishbowl as a main job hunting platform. So people do not use it quite often. So if you text me today that I'm looking for a job, I may see that message next week.

6. So there are not many people there? Exactly. It's growing but not as much as Glassdoor yet.

7. Apart from Fishbowl, what other apps do you use specifically for asking referrals to people? Mostly I would say LinkedIn. LinkedIn is a major platform.

8. Do you have any experience of asking someone on LinkedIn specifically? Yes.

9. Could you walk me through it, how did it happen, what happened when you asked for referral, was the referral successful or did the interview get scheduled? So basically, firstly I went on the specific company's portal or company's website for which I am looking for a role into. And then I went on the company's website on the career page. I looked at some job description and when I think that this particular job description is matching to my resume, I went on LinkedIn. I searched for people working in that particular company and probably HRs or managers who would hire and if they have something in their own projects, or people who are working in similar job profiles. So for example, if I'm looking for a job role in PMO, then I would go on LinkedIn and I would search for HRs, project managers directly, or PMOs in that particular company so that they would know if any similar opening is there. And I'll cold message them. And if they respond, sometimes they do, sometimes they don't, I will talk to them. I will ask them for a referral. They'll take my resume. They would put it on their portal and they'll may come back with the feedback.

10. How many times has it happened that they haven't reverted? A lot of times actually. You can say 80% of the time they don't reply.

11. If you just revisit it and think about it, what was the hardest part of getting a referral? To get the reply actually. Because people... see nobody would want to spend their energy and time on someone they don't know. And if I am to refer somebody, I will do it only if I'm getting something out of it. So usually not everybody gets placed finally in your company. So you don't put that energy for everyone. Out of 5 people only maybe 1 person will get finally placed for which you will get incentive or reward. But other 4 times why would you want to spend your energy. So that is the main gap that I feel that people don't want to go beyond their comfort zone for somebody else who they do not know.

12. So they don't have any incentive to put in the work? Yeah that's what. There is nothing for them in this.

13. And in the scenarios where they did apply your resume in their referral portals, how did you manage it? Were you able to track it? No, so basically sometimes when the employee or the person if they are applying it on the company's portal with my application and everything, so I can go and check my application status. I would get an email from some companies. But a lot of times they do it internally and I'm not sure about the process what they follow. But a lot of times they do it internally and finally they'll come back after two or three weeks that this was not successful. I'm sorry. So I would never know what actually happened. If they ever applied in reality or not I'm not sure. Because there is no way to track it.

14. So like all of them come back and tell you that you haven't been shortlisted or is it only a few people? No. So I am assuming whoever came back did apply or did refer me. But whoever did not reply, they did not refer me. So basically the gap is that I would never know if somebody has really done that. And yeah there is no way to track for me.

15. So for you it's all opaque. You have no way to track it and you basically can't be sure whether they have applied even or not, right? And even if they did you can't track it, there is no way. Yes exactly. I am completely dependent on the person. And as you said only a few companies give an email or something that process has started is it? Some companies have replied me on the email that this referral was not successful, we will not proceed, we will not be able to proceed this time and all, but most of the time I haven't received anything.

16.How do you decide who to ask for a referral? Do you look for credibility signals, or will you ask any employee from the company? I basically judge based on my instincts. If I feel that somebody is replying to me, then maybe they'll be more comfortable helping somebody or helping me in referral. If somebody is not even replying to your first message, then why would they take all this pain going forward.

17. So for LinkedIn it has to be a connection right? If someone is not a connection you can't DM them, correct? Uh sometimes you can, but you cannot start a conversation. You can just send them one message and yeah if they reply then you can continue that conversation. It's not a mandatory thing that you must have a connection to start a conversation.

18. What information do you send while requesting a referral from somebody? I give them my introduction, just a liner or two, and then I tell them that I am interested in this particular position. I give them the job ID. And I give them my updated resume so that they can apply and that's it.

19. And why do you feel the need of asking referrals? If companies are posting jobs on LinkedIn and many other places, why do you feel that referrals are required? I think we all know that referrals work way better than directly applying to the company's portal or applying on Naukri or LinkedIn. Because whenever you are going to apply on some job, there are already too many applicants, who have already applied to that job. So I think HRs do not have that time and energy to go, to view every resume for even more than 10 seconds. So I think basically the number of applicants is so much that referrals are the way I guess.

20.So for you, do you build a connection first? Or do you ask for a referral directly in the first message? See honestly, if I am looking for a job I don't have the time to build a connection with everyone. So I just cold message everyone whoever I think will be suitable for this thing. And whoever replies I just start a conversation with them, who doesn't I just leave it at it.

21.If you were to design a solution to improve referrals, what would actually make your life easier? I think a way so that I can track or I can see if my application has been submitted in the company's portal or in the system basically. A system like that. And I think if I am also the person who is being asked for a referral, I would say that I would also require, you know if I'm doing a favor for somebody, if I get something in return I'll do it quite often. So if a person is placed in my company then only I get rewards from my company, but for other 10 candidates or applicants who did not get placed, I don't get anything for them. So it's like a mental toll for me if I you know go and refer everyone, why would I do that.

22.So it feels like you’re firing arrows in the dark. 10% converted will lead to bonus, and 90% who don’t convert, requires mental toll for submission? Yes, exactly. So you get a reward, you get 5% of reward for 100% of what you have done.

23. And is there any way where you can see that this person has posted a referral for a job specifically? Sorry didn't understand.

24.So have you seen cases where employees post that there’s a referral opening and ask people to DM them? No. I have seen that on Fishbowl that people share a screenshot or something that we have opening in this field or that if you want a referral you can DM me. But it's a screenshot, you don't even know how old it is.

25. Okay so on Fishbowl people do post referral openings? Yeah.

26. On LinkedIn you haven't seen it? No. On Fishbowl and on Glassdoor like I mentioned, we have communities, we have many communities based on companies, based on your interests, based on skill sets, and based on that if you want referral you can follow this community specifically and you can connect with people who are interested in giving referral and connect with people who wants referral basically.

27. So communities are meant to refer or is it general community? They are made for referral purpose only but they are not solely used for that. People when people communicate they just talk anything and everything. So it's not restricted to one thing.

28. And also is there any way to manage multiple referrals? Like suppose you have asked 10 people for referrals. So how do you manage it? Yes. If I have asked them on pings then I will just check pings one by one.

29. So there is no way to manage it at single place right? You have to check one by one. No. So far no.

30. So if I ask you, what would your dream referral process would look like, how would you define it? I would say if I apply, for example if I want to apply in... I have five companies that I am interested in, and I it would be very easy and very convenient for me to have one platform wherein I can apply in all of these companies. And if not directly in these companies, if I am seeking for referral then I would you know feel much better if I have one single place where I can track it at least. So for example, if you see in Naukri, you cannot communicate with people on Naukri, but you can see all the companies that you've applied in. So that's what, if I want a referral for, I can just see in this company, I asked this person referral for.

31. So basically you would like to have a standalone platform specifically for referrals and there should be a feature to track what is the status of your referral right? Anything else you would like to see there which you would love to have? I'm not sure if I will be... because see, when I am referring somebody else, I do it in my company's Workday portal. I'm not sure how that internal Workday portal can be clubbed with some outside application which will show data, because this is organization level data and I'm not sure if companies would be ready to share that.

32.Only if the employee manually updates the status, right? They’d have to coordinate with the hiring team to know what’s happening and then update it on the app. Not exactly. So I can talk about my company. We have a Workday portal and if we refer somebody then we get updates. If we get updates that the interview was taken, first round of interview was taken, it was completed, now it has reached, now it has gone for second round of interview, we get emails.

33. Okay and how many stages have you seen if you have referred? I guess mostly I've seen two.

34. And what are those stages? So first is whenever you refer somebody you get an automated email that your referral submission has been successful. And then you receive an email, then the candidate gets a call by the HR, and then whatever feedback is there, the HR has to update it on the portal. And once they update it on the portal you get the similar automated email that the candidate had a first round of interview or they had a call with HR and thank you for making the submission. And yeah this is the second round and similarly the rest of the rounds are.

35. Okay so first HR takes a screening and then interview is scheduled. So there is a transparency right? You can see all of this through emails. Yes. If I'm not able to check the emails, if I directly want to go to the Workday portal and check the status, that is also possible.

36. Okay, that's all for now. You have been really kind to answer all my questions. Thanks a lot. No problem Devansh, it's been a pleasure.
