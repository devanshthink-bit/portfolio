3. Basically an obligation, right? Ah, kind of. So I go to the portal. I firstly ask them to share the job ID because it is not practically possible for me to find the job ID. So whoever needs the referral, the applicant must share the job ID that they are looking for the job into. And once they share the job ID and their updated resume, I go to the Workday portal that we have at Accenture. And I submit all their details, for example, their name, phone number. I have to have them on call or at least inform them online because there are a lot of details that won't be there in the resume. Email, phone number. Email is usually there and phone number, but the city, the city choice that they are looking job into, there are other details that I need while submitting the referral for someone else. So all these details once they are filled, then I submit it, and I and the applicant both receive an automated email that thank you for applying in this job, our hiring executive will reach out to you shortly as soon as possible and will let you know the process. So every time I have referred somebody, every time they have received a call back. There's not been a single case when the person hasn't received anything. So once they receive the call, the HR will mostly check with the candidate if they are fine with the location, if they're fine with the job description, have they gone through it, other basic details. And once that call is completed, Me and the candidate both are sent an automated email saying that we had an initial connect with the applicant, and it was successful, we'll now proceed with the resume screening. If it is shortlisted, they will be proceeded with the first round of interview. So then the first round of interview happens, and if the first round of interview is cleared, then the HR will accordingly reach out to the candidate for the second round and yeah.
