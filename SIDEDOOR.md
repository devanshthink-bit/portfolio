# Sidedoor case study (branch: sidedoor)

Work on the Sidedoor case study happens on this branch, in this folder.

- `main` is the live portfolio sent to recruiters. Nothing here reaches it until this branch is merged.
- Every push here gets a Vercel preview URL, not production.
- Pull live fixes in from `main` with `git merge main`.
- When the case study is finished, merge `sidedoor` into `main` to launch it.
